    The Meaning of the Universe

    A 3D research library organized as a galaxy. Each star is a domain of inquiry. Each planet is a research environment that distills its corpus into a conclusion you can read in a minute — or descend into for hours.

    The library begins with eight territories spanning the metaphysical, physical, systemic, and human: Simulation Theory · Plasma Dynamics · World Religions · Economics · Esoterica · Astrophysics · Cosmology · Computation.

    The Librarian — bring your own Claude API key — can help you navigate, summarize, and propose adjacent reading. Click the chip or press ? to open. Click the mic to speak; replies are spoken back in your chosen voice.
